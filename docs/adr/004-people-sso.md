# People identity bridge

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: Proposed

Evaluate an identity bridge between employee subjects and customer accounts. Unresolved: issuer, subject lifecycle, offboarding behavior, and who approves a link. Payroll is mentioned as a potential consumer but has no signed interface. The properties-based read lookup can proceed separately. This proposal must not be interpreted as an approved SSO rollout.

Review changes against the relevant service implementation and release records. The decision applies to the named component and environment; it does not establish ownership of similarly named modules elsewhere in the repository.
