# Engineering Journal publishing

Editorial · 2026-09-18

The journal is the blog workspace. Articles are maintained in the Remix application and reviewed by Editorial. Shared typography and Link components come from the web UI package.

Publication does not require the commerce gateway, Accounts, or People Directory. The editorial team uses terms such as user research and inventory of articles in planning discussions, but these are publishing concepts rather than service dependencies.

The journal can be started by itself on port 3003. Analytics integration was removed from the local distribution. The editorial lab route is a static page used to try small layout ideas before a future navigation refresh.
