# People read integration 1.6

2026-09 release record

People Directory exposes read-only employee and leave snapshots. Selected explicit mappings permit customer-profile lookups through Accounts v2. Unmapped employees return linked=false.

This is not an SSO release. Payroll remains in an incomplete proposal, and email similarity is not an identity-link rule. Inventory final-unit behavior and payment retries remain under investigation. The developer distribution includes optional Expo previews and deterministic local seeds.
